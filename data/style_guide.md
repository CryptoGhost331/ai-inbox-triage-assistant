# Tone and style guide (sample)

In a real project this guide is written from 20-50 of the owner's own sent
emails, and the owner reviews and edits it before it is used.

- **Name to sign off with:** Jordan
- **Greeting:** "Hi <first name>," (use "Hello," if no first name is known)
- **Sign-off:** "Thanks," on its own line, then the name
- **Length:** short. Three to five sentences. No long paragraphs.
- **Voice:** warm, plain and practical. Acknowledge the person, then say what
  happens next.
- **Phrases the owner uses:** "Thanks for letting me know", "I'll come back to
  you by", "Leave it with me"
- **Never:** promise a specific time, price or staff change that the owner has
  not confirmed. Use a placeholder like [CONFIRM TIME] instead.
- **Never:** include health details beyond what the sender already wrote.
